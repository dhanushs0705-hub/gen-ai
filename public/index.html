Platform Walkthrough
Step 1 of 4
1. Real-Time Transaction Monitoring
The platform ingests live financial transactions (UPI, NetBanking, RuPay, Card swipes) in Indian Rupees (₹). Each payment is processed in under 15 milliseconds and categorized into Approved (Green), Manual Review (Amber), or Blocked (Red).
How to use: Use the filter pills (All, Cleared, Review, Declined) above the table to view specific transaction statuses, or click "Step" to manually advance.
2. Deep-Dive Inspection & Explainable AI (SHAP)
Click on any transaction row in the table to open the inspection panel. You will see the exact Ensemble Risk Score (0-100), Isolation Forest anomaly index, and a clear SHAP waterfall explaining every risk factor in plain English.
Transparency: No black-box decisions — every alert shows exact evidence (amount deviations, velocity spikes, foreign VPNs, or device changes).
3. Test Targeted Fraud Attacks
At the bottom of the feed, click any of the 4 simulation buttons:
  • Card Testing: 5 rapid micro-charges (₹10) probing card validity.
  • Impossible Travel: London swipe 10 mins after Mumbai swipe (57,000 km/h).
  • Account Takeover: ₹3.85 Lakh transfer at 3 AM from a foreign VPN.
  • Crypto Drain: Sudden ₹6.2 Lakh liquidation to an offshore wallet.
Visual alert: Notice how impossible travel triggers an interactive geodesic flight arc with the exact required speed.
4. Custom Sandbox & Bank Policy Rules
Click "Evaluate transaction" in the top bar to test any custom payment scenario. Click "Policy rules" to dynamically adjust the bank's automated block threshold (default: 80) and 10-minute velocity limits in real time.
